Trustworthy AI · Federated Learning · Distributed Systems

My research focuses on building machine-learning systems that remain trustworthy when data and computation are distributed. I study fairness, privacy, and system-level challenges in federated and decentralized learning.

Research

Research agenda

Central question. How can distributed AI systems provide useful models while controlling fairness and privacy risks under heterogeneous data, participants, and system conditions?
01 · Fairness

Fairness in Federated Learning

Characterizing how data heterogeneity, client behavior, data selection, and mitigation strategies affect global and local fairness.

02 · Privacy

Privacy-Preserving ML

Studying membership-inference risks and adaptive defenses for federated learning.

03 · Systems

Distributed AI Systems

Designing and evaluating scalable systems for decentralized and federated learning.
