Trustworthy AI · Federated Learning · Privacy · Fairness

Building fair, privacy-preserving, and high-performing distributed AI systems.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from INSA Lyon, where I conducted research at the LIRIS laboratory within the DRIM research group, under the supervision of Prof. Sara Bouchenak. My research studies how decentralized AI can remain accurate while providing stronger fairness and privacy guarantees.

My interests include fair, private and trustworthy AI; resilient and highly available distributed systems; and the ethical, societal and regulatory implications of AI and distributed technologies.
